Welcome to Key Stakeholder Roles in E B P Implementation. In the next five minutes, you will learn how behavioral health practitioners, counties, Centers of Excellence, and Department of Health Care Services contribute to implementing evidence-based practices and how their responsibilities differ.

Evidence-based practice, or E B P, implementation involves four key stakeholder groups. Each group has distinct responsibilities for supporting implementation, meeting requirements, and delivering services.

Behavioral health practitioners complete required training, meet applicable quality requirements, deliver E B Ps, and report required data. Counties coordinate implementation, ensure practitioners meet requirements, and submit Medi-Cal claims and individual and service-level encounters to D H C S. Centers of Excellence develop implementation resources and provide training, technical assistance, and quality support. D H C S establishes requirements and operational guidance, reviews data, oversees compliance, and addresses policy and payment questions.

Together, these four roles form a connected system. D H C S establishes requirements and provides oversight. Centers of Excellence, or C O Es, provide training, technical assistance, and quality support. Counties ensure their practitioners meet applicable requirements. Practitioners complete the requirements and deliver E B Ps.

Training
DHCS
Set requirements; oversee compliance.
COEs
Develop, deliver & track trainings; send reports.

Ensure training is completed; review reports.
Practitioners
Complete required trainings.
Fidelity Monitoring
DHCS
Set requirements; review statewide data.
COEs
Conduct assessments; coordinate scheduling; send reports.
Counties
Ensure timelines are met; review reports.
Practitioners
Complete fidelity assessments on schedule.
Service Delivery
DHCS
Set guidance; oversee compliance.
COEs
Support quality service delivery for practitioners & counties.
Counties
Ensure service delivery meets requirements.
Practitioners
Enroll eligible individuals; deliver EBPs to standard.
